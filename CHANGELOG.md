# Changelog

## 0.2.0-beta.10 – not yet released

- The quests Forever added say where to look: which creatures carry what
  is wanted, or where it lies, for 31 quests that only named the errand
  (the Kor Gem's naga, the Crest of Lordaeron's hiding places, the book in
  Dalaran, Tunk's moulding and more).
- Errands done mostly out in the world are no longer listed under a
  dungeon because a creature inside carries the same thing: Crocolisk
  Hunting (Loch Modan's crocolisks) under the Wailing Caverns, the Thick
  Leather turn-ins, Metzen the Reindeer, Indurium, An Earnest Proposition
  and some ten more.
- A Moon-Kissed Blade is listed under each dungeon one of its pieces comes
  from: the Deadmines, Shadowfang Keep and Blackfathom Deeps.
- Blackfathom Deeps' map no longer runs ahead: the naga and satyrs of the
  first sheet uncovered part of the second, and the Moonshrine's cultists
  the whole of the third, long before you got there. Each sheet now opens
  when you reach it, as on a real run.
- Naxxramas: spiders, maggots and bats no longer uncover the quarters they
  do not live in.
- Molten Core: beating Sulfuron no longer uncovers Golemagg's and
  Majordomo's halls (nor Lucifron's cavern the halls past Gehennas); its
  wings are taken in any order, and each is uncovered on its own.
- Uldaman: reaching the Map Chamber no longer uncovers Dig Two, the dead
  end where the Lost Dwarves wait.
- Temple of Ahn'Qiraj: the way down into the Vault of C'Thun, which the
  game gives no name the journal could hear, is uncovered once the Twin
  Emperors are faced; it stayed blank before.
- The journal opened outside a dungeon no longer says its pages are blank
  when they are not: it opens on a contents page listing every place
  written about, with how far each has got. "Contents" at the top of the
  list goes back to it.
- A boss's page says when it was last defeated and how often you fell to
  it, and sets each find against its victories ("found twice in 5
  victories").
- A boss whose name the game hid as the fight began is given its name when
  the fight ends, instead of staying "Unknown".
- Item tooltips say where the journal noted an item and who carried it
  ("Field Notes: found in the Deadmines, from Cookie, 2 times"), in your
  bags, chat links and loot rolls alike, and the reward of one of a
  dungeon's quests says which. Preferences → "Finds on item tooltips"
  turns it off.
- Taking up or handing in a quest that leads into a dungeon goes in that
  dungeon's diary (quietly, when you are elsewhere).
- The Quests page puts the quests you carry first, those ready to hand in
  at the top, then those still to be taken up, and those done last, each
  under its own heading. A quest you are too young for says from what
  level it can be taken up.
- A dungeon's first page lists your visits: when, for how long, at what
  level, and what came of each (foes defeated, falls, finds). Visits from
  before this version are read from the diary (without the level).
- Loot seen only in chat just after a boss the game announces no fight for
  (Cookie, Lorgus Jett) is now credited to that boss, as for any other.
- A boss's page lists the quests that send you after it (or after those it
  fights beside), and whether you carry them; click one for the Quests
  page. A creature in the bestiary says which unfinished quests want it.
- "/fn find <words>" searches the journal: every boss, creature, find,
  hall, quest and note of your own that holds the words, each a click away
  from its page.
- The minimap button's tooltip says how far the place you are in has got.
- A long diary shows its latest 60 entries and a line to read the rest.
- Notes of your own on quests: click a quest's title on the Quests page.
- A boss's mark on the map says how often it fell and how swiftly.
- A raid or dungeon you are saved to says so on its first page: until
  when, and how many of its foes are down, as the game's raid information
  has it.
- A page that fails to draw is kept for "/fn errors" instead of raising an
  error on screen.
- Creatures that are called up in a fight rather than standing in the
  dungeon (Arugal's Voidwalkers, Aku'mai Servants, drakonids, Hakkari
  Minions and some 270 more) have their level, kind and portrait in the
  bestiary.
- Each visit notes who went along: the names of your group, including
  those who joined on the way ("with Brann and Thrall"). "/fn find" finds
  them too.
- A quest whose pieces are gathered in two or three dungeons is listed
  under each, with a word on where the rest is found: The Test of
  Righteousness (the Deadmines and Shadowfang Keep), the Orb of Soran'ruk,
  Three Kings of Flame, Atiesh and some 20 more.
- Blackfathom Deeps: Gelihast's finds are listed under his fight (which the
  game calls "Geilhast") instead of "From the halls"; journals that already
  hold them are put right. The same goes for any boss whose fight is named
  otherwise than the creature.
- The creatures of the new Forever dungeons (Blackmaw Druids, Alcaz
  Jailers, Drowned Hexxers, Krol'dok Brutes, Ravasaur Hunters and some 440
  more) have a portrait and their kind in the bestiary, from Wowhead's
  listing. Their levels mostly stay blank: those dungeons scale.
- Portraits for Ezra Grimm, Hans and Greta, the Ravasaur Hunters and two
  more bosses that had none.
- Abilities for nine bosses that had none, from the lists Wowhead read out
  of the game: Alcaz Prison's four (Overseer Wrex, Tidemistress Ca'zass,
  The Questioner, Blazeroar), the Butcher and the Lordaeron Captain in the
  Ruins of Lordaeron, Impaan the Imprisoner and Elder Shaman Bloodmane in
  Blackmaw Hold, and Dalaran's Mana Wraith. The Mana Echoes, Phantasms,
  Arcane Elementals, Remnants and Fragmented Sentries of Dalaran, and
  Blackmaw Hold's Berserkers, Legashi and Felfire Imps, have theirs in the
  bestiary.
- Seventeen more quests say whom to look for, where what they ask for is
  carried by many: Hearts of Zeal (Scarlet Myrmidons, Monks...), Deviate
  Hides, Red Silk Bandanas, Troll Temper, Dark Iron Fanny Packs, Ogre
  Warbeads and more, the likeliest carriers first; the map marks where they
  live.
- A creature in the bestiary names the quests you carry before the others
  that want it.
- Quests whose quarry stands outside a dungeon, by its way in, say so:
  Techbot, Mad Magglish, Magregan Deepshadow, the Common Hall chests of
  Uldaman, the Sapphires of Aku'Mai, the Shadowshards of Maraudon and the
  Razorfen outside Razorfen Downs among them. Uldaman Reagent Run names the
  Magenta Cap Clusters, and Kibler's errands and Mother's Milk in Blackrock
  Spire whom they want; the Cap Clusters and the Spire Spider Eggs are
  marked on the map.
- Who gives a quest reads "the Sparklematic 5200" mid-sentence.
- "Find OOX-17/TN!" and "Crystal in the Mountains" are no longer listed
  under Zul'Farrak and Razorfen Downs: what begins them drops all over.
- Creatures called up as pets (BFD's Voidwalker Minions) no longer show
  the level of a namesake elsewhere.
- Places read as they are said: "errands that lead into the Deadmines",
  "set foot in the Wailing Caverns".
- Eleven quests whose quarry the database leaves unnamed say whom to look
  for: the pieces of Lord Valthalak's Amulet, The Restless Souls, Dead
  Man's Plea, A Taste of Flame and more.
- Some 90 more creatures have their level, kind and portrait: those whose
  spawn point holds one of several kinds (Shadowfang Keep's Shadow
  Chargers among them). Quest marks for them are placed too.
- The map no longer lists the dungeon's own name ("The Deadmines") among
  the halls not yet placed on it.
- Baron Aquanis in Blackfathom Deeps (summoned at the Fathom Stone, with no
  encounter of the game's) lists his own Frost Nova and Frostbolt, not the
  Depth Charge and Bubble Beam of the Season of Discovery raid's Baron.
- "/fn find" also searches the diaries ("/fn find fell": every time you fell
  in battle, and where), after everything else it finds; and the known
  abilities of the bosses you have beaten and the creatures you have met
  ("/fn find frostbolt"), and the quests that send you after what you
  searched for ("/fn find ravager": Deviate Hides, Deviate Eradication) or
  that the one searched for gives ("/fn find nalpak").
- "/fn find <words>" typed before the journal had been opened once since
  logging in raised a Lua error; it opens the search page now.
- Known abilities no longer list a fight's hidden workings: "Kirtonos
  Gargoyle Passive" on every Scholomance boss, "Shade of Eranikus Passive
  Visual", Heigan's "Plague Wave Controller", "Reset Gothik Event"; and in
  the bestiary, "Invisibility and Stealth Detection" on 13 creatures and
  the like.
- "/fn quality" alone says which items are being noted, instead of listing
  every command.
- A dungeon whose halls the game never names (Shadowfang Keep) no longer
  says "Nothing noted yet" under "Halls and chambers" once you have been
  through it: it says the map is charted by the foes you meet.
- The contents page no longer says "all 3 foes of note defeated" of the
  Wailing Caverns, which has eight: just "3 foes of note defeated".
- A boss's page counts the times you fell to it from before this version
  too, read from the diary ("fallen to it once" on Arugal, say).

## 0.2.0-beta.9

- Quests show what they pay: the items to choose from and those given
  regardless, with their tooltips.
- A quest handed out inside a dungeon marks its giver on the map until you
  take it up.
- The quests of the dungeons new to Forever say who gives them.
- A quest whose objectives are met no longer marks the map, and the journal
  keeps up with the quest log while it lies open.
- Quest marks sit exactly on their spot, stepping aside only for a boss's
  skull.
- Bosses the game announces no fight for (Lorgus Jett in Blackfathom Deeps)
  get a page and a skull on the map once killed; they used to show nowhere.
  Kills already noted are made good.
- A quest target that may be in one of several places (Lorgus Jett) is
  marked at every one of them.
- A quest ready to hand in inside the dungeon marks who to take it to.
- More quests: those that begin from an item a boss drops (Baron Aquanis),
  those whose quarry is summoned, a class's own quests (shown to that class
  only), and thirteen quests Forever added to the old dungeons. 449 quests
  across 37 instances.
- A quest that moves along while you are inside a dungeon is written down
  under it, whatever the game files it under.
- Baron Aquanis is marked at the Fathom Stone, where he rises when the
  Fathom Core is taken, and his quests say so.
- A quest you hold no longer says who gives it, and one that is ready no
  longer says what to look for.
- Rewards the game has not shown you yet are asked for, so their names
  fill in.

## 0.2.0-beta.8

- Portraits for the bosses of the dungeons new to Forever (82 of them),
  drawn from the creature itself by its id.
- Quests: each dungeon and raid has a Quests page listing the quests that
  lead into it (your side's only), what each asks, who to look for, and
  whether you carry it or have finished it. 389 quests across 35 instances,
  the nine dungeons new to Forever among them.
- Quests on the map: a mark where each unfinished quest leads (the boss's
  place, or the room its creatures keep), brighter for quests in your log.
  It can be turned off in Preferences.
- A quest in your log shows how far along it is, in the game's own words
  ("0/1 Fathom Core"), and "ready to hand in" once it is.
- Quests also say what to open or use (the Fathom Stone for the Fathom
  Core), and the map marks the very spot where the game's data gives one.
- Quests your log files under a dungeon that the journal had not heard of
  are written down and kept.
- Each quest says who gives it (and whether they stand inside the dungeon),
  the item that begins it, and the quest it follows.

## 0.2.0-beta.7

- Known abilities for the bosses of the dungeons new to Forever: most of
  Dalaran, the Ruins of Lordaeron, the Hall of Thanes, the Wetlands
  Excavation Site, the Drowned City and Krol'dok Stronghold, and some of
  Blackmaw Hold. Taken from the Warcraft Wiki's boss pages and published
  guides (Method, Warcraft Tavern, Fextralife and others) where they agree,
  and from the game's own spell text where it names the boss; every spell
  was checked against the game's data. Alcaz Prison and the Shaper's Terrace
  remain mostly unwritten: nothing is published on them yet.
- Also filled in: Azgaloth, Old Serra'kis, Grubbis, Viscous Fallout.

## 0.2.0-beta.6

- Portraits for bosses that are summoned or step out of another creature
  (Sneed, Mutanus, Nefarian, Ragnaros and some forty more); Sneed's page also
  pictures his shredder.
- Fights against a company (the Twin Emperors, the Four Horsemen, the Seven,
  the Lost Dwarves...) picture each member by name.
- The notes box follows the text size chosen in Preferences.

## 0.2.0-beta.5

- Portraits: each boss page and bestiary entry shows the creature, drawn from
  the game's own model as a sepia sketch or in true colour, animated or held
  still, or not at all (Preferences).
- Your own notes on single entries: click a creature in the bestiary, an item
  in the spoils or a hall on a dungeon's first page, and the notes box writes
  on that entry; the note shows under it.
- "See-through" now thins the paper and the window behind it and leaves the
  writing solid.
- Shadowfang Keep's later sheets and the Deadmines docks follow the order of
  real runs, so the map no longer runs ahead there.
- Friendly characters (captives, quest givers) are never filed as creatures,
  and trash first met during a boss fight returns to the bestiary once it is
  killed on its own.


## 0.2.0-beta.4

- Maps for the eight new Forever dungeons that had none: Blackmaw Hold, The
  Drowned City, The Hall of Thanes, City of Dalaran, Ruins of Lordaeron,
  Kroldok Stronghold, The Shaper's Terrace and the Excavation Site, painted in
  the game's map style from their minimaps. Room and boss placements on these
  are first guesses.
- The journal can be resized by dragging its bottom right corner, or made to
  fill the screen (the button beside the close button, `/fn full`, or
  Preferences).
- Fuller boss ability lists: fights that the server scripts in code (Arugal's
  curse and teleport, Onyxia, Ragnaros, Nefarian and 85 others) now list what
  those scripts cast.
- More in Preferences: the size of the writing, how light the paper is, how
  see-through the window is, and the size of the boss marks on the map.
- Dusts, essences and shards from disenchanting are no longer noted as loot.
- Blackfathom Deeps, Scarlet Monastery and Naxxramas: creatures are filed by
  where they really stand, as in the single-road dungeons.

## 0.2.0-beta.3

- A Preferences page at the bottom of the journal's contents (`/fn settings`,
  or right-click the minimap button): every option by clicking, plus the size
  of the journal window.
- Spoils are listed under the boss that carried them, then the rest.
- The journal remembers where you dragged it.
- Shadowfang Keep no longer uncovers rooms before you reach them, and in the
  other single-road dungeons creatures are filed by where they really stand.
- The map opens on the sheet you were last on, so boss marks on later sheets
  are not missed.
- A darker parchment look: the pages use the same sheet as the maps, with
  dark brown ink and red-brown headings.

## 0.2.0-beta.2

- Newly charted parts of a map are sketched in, room after room along the
  road, instead of appearing at once; a newly faced boss's marker is inked in
  after its room. New entries on the other pages (areas, bosses, bestiary,
  spoils, abilities) fade in the same way. `/fn sketch` turns this off.
- A slip of parchment shows on screen when something new is written down;
  click it to open the journal, drag it to move it (`/fn notices move`).
- The journal opens, closes and turns pages with sound (`/fn sound`).
- Your own deaths are written in the diary.
- The map key works in combat.
- Friendly characters who speak during a fight no longer enter the bestiary.
- Only "receives loot" chat lines count as loot: not rolls, not crafted items.

## 0.2.0-beta.1 – first public test build

Everything is new to testers; this lists what the build contains and what
changed since the private 0.1.0.

### The journal
- Pages per dungeon: overview with diary, bosses, map, bestiary, spoils, and
  your own notes on each dungeon and boss.
- Maps for 27 instances, uncovered area by area; single-road dungeons fill in
  behind you; raid wings and hub sheets are handled per wing.
- Boss markers on the maps (253 of 256 bosses placed), clickable.
- Shipped lore: abilities for 299 boss encounters and level, type and
  abilities for 1,131 dungeon creatures, shown only after you have met them.
- The map key opens the journal's map inside dungeons (`/fn mapkey`).

### Since 0.1.0
- The bestiary works on the Forever client: creatures are recorded from
  experience messages, looted corpses and what they say in combat, since the
  game hides their names and ids from addons.
- Loot is credited to the corpse it came from; trash drops no longer land on
  boss pages.
- Bosses stay out of the bestiary, including ones the addon has no data for.
- Creatures killed just before a boss pull are no longer filed under the boss.
- Errors are caught instead of interrupting play, kept, and shown by
  `/fn errors`.
- A key binding (Keybindings → AddOns), `/fn version`, a first-run chat line.

### Known gaps
- No maps yet for eight of the new Forever dungeons (Blackmaw Hold, The
  Drowned City, The Hall of Thanes, City of Dalaran, Ruins of Lordaeron,
  Kroldok Stronghold, The Shaper's Terrace, Excavation Site).
- Three bosses have no map marker (Mechanical Menagerie, The Molten Core
  encounter, Ezra Grimm).
- Boss marker positions for Alcaz Prison and upper Shadowfang Keep are guesses.
- Only played in the Deadmines so far; everything else is checked by an
  offline simulation, not in the game.
- English client only.
